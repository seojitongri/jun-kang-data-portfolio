## Executive Summary

This project analyzes reservation data from a two-location karaoke business 
(Berkeley and Oakland) to understand demand patterns, capacity utilization, 
and operational inefficiencies.

Key findings:
- Peak demand occurs between 8–10 PM across both locations
- Oakland shows stronger reservation-based demand, while Berkeley relies heavily on walk-ins
- Significant underutilized capacity exists during early hours

Business impact:
- Identified opportunities for targeted promotions and pricing strategies
- Highlighted need for improved data tracking and reservation systems
